About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles. We prepare professionals to operate in regulated and high‑consequence environments where security, resilience and governance are critical.

IDR bridges academic insight and real-world industry needs through collaboration with university partners and organisations. Our programs combine practical training, research insight and live project experience to build the next generation of digital risk practitioners.

Our Model

Academy

Training programs and bootcamps designed for students and professionals entering digital, cyber and technology risk careers.

Innovation & Incubation

Development of new risk models, AI governance frameworks and intellectual property supporting the future of digital risk.

Advisory Services

Helping organisations implement leading frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2 to strengthen digital resilience.
