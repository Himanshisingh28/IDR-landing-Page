# Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) – Homepage Design

## Project Overview

This project is a simple responsive homepage design for the **Institute of Digital Risk ** **IDR**, an industry-led institute focused on training and deploying professionals in digital, cyber, and AI risk management.

The website introduces IDR’s mission, services, and community while maintaining a clean and modern technology-focused design.

---

## Features

* Responsive homepage (works on desktop and mobile)
* Cube-inspired SVG logo design
* Clean UI using an orange, black, and white color palette
* Semantic HTML5 structure
* Responsive layout using CSS Grid and Flexbox
* Contact / register interest form

---

## Website Sections

1. **Hero Section**

   * Headline presenting IDR’s mission
   * Short description
   * Call-to-action button

2. **About IDR**

   * Overview of the institute
   * Focus on digital, cyber, and technology risk roles
   * Emphasis on academic and industry collaboration

3. **Service Pillars**

   * Academy – training and bootcamps
   * Innovation & Incubation – risk models and AI governance
   * Advisory Services – implementation of frameworks like NIST and ISO 27001

4. **Community**

   * Students
   * Early-career professionals
   * Practitioners from regulated sectors

5. **Contact**

   * Simple form to register interest or contact IDR

## Logo Design Explanation

The logo uses a **cube-inspired geometric shape** to represent structure, resilience, and layered digital risk systems.
The **orange color** symbolizes innovation and technology, while **black and white** provide contrast, professionalism, and clarity.
The design is minimalist and scalable, ensuring it remains clear even at small sizes such as favicons or mobile headers.
